\# Skill: Generate Weekly Briefing



\## Trigger

When requested or on scheduled Sunday night



\## Steps

1\. Read Business\_Goals.md for targets

2\. Count all files in /Done from past 7 days

3\. Read /Logs for past 7 days

4\. Generate briefing in /Briefings named YYYY-MM-DD\_Monday\_Briefing.md

5\. Include: Executive Summary, Tasks Completed, Bottlenecks, Proactive Suggestions, Upcoming Deadlines
